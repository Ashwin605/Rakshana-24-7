FAQ

Common Questions v10.1 LIVE

Find answers to the most common questions about our proactive safety shield.

Is my data really anonymous?

Yes. All personal information like phone numbers and facial data is one-way hashed and encrypted using AES-256 before analysis. We do not store raw files. Your identity is shielded even from our own servers.

How does the NLP scanning work?

The threat scorer doesn't just look at keywords; it evaluates context. It distinguishes between a friendly mention and malicious doxxing, accurately mapping threats to laws like the IT Act and IPC. It understands sarcasm, slang, and indirect threats.

Does this replace police reports?

No. Rakshana acts as your early warning system and evidence collector. The 'Report' feature helps you securely and anonymously submit this evidence directly to the National Cyber Crime portal. We help you build a stronger case by collecting evidence before it is deleted.
