LEGAL REFERENCE

Know Your Rights.
Know the Law.

Every Indian woman has legal protection against cyber crimes. Here is every law, section, and helpline you need — in plain language.

YOUR CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS

Rights Every Woman Has Online

These rights are guaranteed by the Constitution of India and enforced by law. No one can take them away.

Right to Privacy

Article 21 of the Constitution. Your personal data, images, conversations, and location are protected. No one can share them without your consent.

Right to Dignity

Article 21 protects your dignity. Online harassment, morphing, stalking, and defamation are violations of this fundamental right.

Right to File FIR

Police MUST register your FIR. If they refuse, approach the Superintendent of Police or file through cybercrime.gov.in directly.

Right to Zero FIR

You can file an FIR at ANY police station in India — not just your local one. This is called a Zero FIR. The police must accept it.

INDIAN CYBER LAW

Laws That Protect You

These are the specific legal sections that apply to cyber crimes against women in India. Screenshot this page — it's your legal toolkit.

IPC / BNS

Cyber Stalking

Section 354D IPC — Stalking | Section 78 BNS (New Law)

If someone repeatedly contacts you, monitors your internet or phone usage, watches or follows you using electronic means, or tracks your movements — this is a criminal offence.

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment + fine (first offence). Up to 5 years for repeat offenders.

IT ACT

Morphed / Manipulated Images & Deepfakes

Section 66E IT Act — Privacy Violation | Section 67/67A — Obscene Content

Publishing or sharing someone's morphed, manipulated, or AI-generated (deepfake) intimate images is a serious criminal offence. This includes creating, distributing, or even possessing such content.

Punishment: Section 66E: Up to 3 years + ₹2 lakh fine. Section 67A: Up to 7 years + ₹10 lakh fine.
Deepfakes Morphed photos Revenge porn AI-generated
IPC / BNS

Online Harassment & Abuse

Section 354A IPC — Sexual Harassment | Section 509 IPC — Insult to Modesty

Sending unwanted sexual messages, lewd comments, threats, or abusive content online is punishable. This covers DMs, comments, emails, and any electronic communication.

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment + fine.
Social media DMs Comments Emails
IPC / BNS

Cyber Blackmail & Sextortion

Section 384/385 IPC — Extortion | Section 506 IPC — Criminal Intimidation

Threatening to publish intimate images or private information unless demands (money, favours) are met is criminal extortion. The threat alone is punishable, even without execution.

Punishment: Up to 7 years imprisonment + fine for extortion. Up to 2 years for criminal intimidation.
Sextortion Blackmail Threats Intimate images
IT ACT

Doxxing — Leaking Personal Information

Section 66C IT Act — Identity Theft | Section 72 IT Act — Breach of Confidentiality

Publishing someone's personal information (address, phone, photos, workplace) online without consent — especially to harass or endanger — is a criminal offence under multiple sections.

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹1 lakh fine.
Doxxing Personal info Identity theft Phone numbers
